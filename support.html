DisplayPreset Support

DisplayPreset is a focused macOS utility for saving and restoring display profiles.

Getting started

Open DisplayPreset with the displays you want to save connected. Review the detected displays, choose Save, and give the profile a name. Select a saved profile whenever you want to restore it.

Restore purchases

Open the unlock screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the same App Store account that made the purchase. Apple may take a moment to return the purchase status.

Common questions

A display is missing. Confirm that macOS itself shows the display, then reconnect the cable or adapter and try again. A profile cannot restore a display that macOS does not currently identify.

Can I move profiles to another Mac? Export a JSON backup, move it securely, and import it on the other Mac.

Where is my purchase? Purchases are tied to your App Store account. Use Restore Purchases rather than buying again.

Contact us

Please include your macOS version, DisplayPreset version, display models, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Email support@displayset.app.
