Help with DisplayPreset

DisplayPreset is a macOS utility for saving display profiles and window layouts.

Accessibility permission

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and enable DisplayPreset. Return to the app and try applying the layout again.

Restore purchases

Open the in-app purchase panel and choose Restore Purchases. DisplayPreset uses non-consumable purchases tied to your Apple account.

Contact

Email support@luckyxmobile.com with your macOS version and a description of the issue. Please do not include private purchase receipts.
