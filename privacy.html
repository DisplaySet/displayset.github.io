DisplayPreset Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Data collection

DisplayPreset does not collect, sell, or share personal data. Display profiles and window layouts are stored locally on your Mac.

Permissions

DisplayPreset may use macOS Accessibility permission to inspect and reposition application windows. The permission is controlled by macOS and is used only for the requested workspace feature.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. DisplayPreset does not receive your payment information.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@luckyxmobile.com.
