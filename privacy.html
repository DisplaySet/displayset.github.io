Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Local data

DisplayPreset stores display profiles and app preferences locally on your Mac. A profile may contain display identity information and the display configuration needed to restore it.

No account or service required

DisplayPreset does not require an account and does not send your profiles to a server. We do not operate analytics, advertising, or tracking in the app.

Files you choose

When you use JSON import or export, the app reads or writes only the file and location you select. You control where exported profiles are stored and who receives them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and your App Store account. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase status is checked locally by the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@displayset.app.
